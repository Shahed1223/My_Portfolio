About me

I am a veterinarian and early-career researcher focused on understanding how parasites, vectors, and hosts interact across ecological, molecular, and computational levels. My research direction spans mosquito ecology, insecticide resistance, neuro-immune regulation, and pathogen transmission dynamics along with molecular identification of parasites in livestock and wildlife systems.

Beyond wet-lab and field research, I work with molecular diagnostics, comparative genomics, in silico analysis, and computational tools to explore parasite genetics, host responses, and biomarker discovery. My broader interests extend into ecology and evolutionary biology, One Health epidemiology, and precision agriculture; integrating environmental and biological data to improve disease surveillance and control strategies.

I aim to build a research career at the intersection of parasitology, vector biology, neurology, molecular & computational biology, and public health
